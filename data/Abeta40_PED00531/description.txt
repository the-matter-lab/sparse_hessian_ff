Abeta40 (amyloid-beta 1-40) is a 40-residue peptide produced from the amyloid precursor protein (APP).
It is a widely studied intrinsically disordered peptide related to Alzheimer's disease and can adopt both compact and extended conformations in solution.

This dataset was downloaded from the Protein Ensemble Database (PED), entry PED00531:
https://proteinensemble.org/entries/PED00531

The ensemble corresponds to a reweighted A beta 40 conformational ensemble with 2998 models.
The XYZ files in this folder were selected as representative compact-to-extended conformers from that larger ensemble.
